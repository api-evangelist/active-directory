Microsoft introduced Frontier Tuning, a new approach enabling organizations to customize AI systems using reinforcement learning within their own compliance boundaries, combining a continuously evolving learning environment with company-specific data and workflows. Early adopters including Pearson, EY, and Bristol Myers Squibb have reported significant improvements, with one organization increasing successful task completion from 13% to 87%. The technology produces tuned models that improve agent performance by teaching AI systems to align with specific organizational operations and processes.
