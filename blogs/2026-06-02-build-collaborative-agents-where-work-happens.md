Microsoft is expanding its Teams platform to make it easier for developers to build and deploy AI agents that function as active team members, including general availability of the Teams SDK in Python, JavaScript, and C#. New interaction patterns allow agents to send targeted messages, use slash commands, and maintain long-running workflows through agent sessions. Partners like GitHub, Linear, and Atlassian are already demonstrating practical applications in Teams across chats, channels, and meetings.
